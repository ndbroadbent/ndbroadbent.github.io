A Bayesian Analysis of Biblical Prophesies

Nathan Broadbent

16 Nov 2025 — 10 min read

I grew up in a Christian family, so I would often think about the end times and prophesies in Revelation while I was trying to fall asleep (it didn't help.)

Every generation seems to find a way to predict that the end of the world is near. From Hitler to the Cold War, and barcodes to the internet, there's always something that lines up with prophesies in the book of Revelation.

However, some of these prophesies do seem to line up particularly well with current events:

I was curious to see how well Donald Trump actually matched these prophesies compared to other public figures, so I did what any normal person would do: I built a Bayesian reasoning framework and I calculated the answer.

Here's the code if you want to take a look at the data and script:

git clone https://github.com/ndbroadbent/bayesian_inference.git
cd bayesian_inference
python antichrist_analysis.py

To add your own subjects, drop a YAML file into data/subjects/ following the existing examples. Then run the script again and see where your candidate lands on the Great Antichrist Leaderboard.

How antichrist_analysis.py works

  1. Loads all the prophecies
    From Daniel, Revelation, Thessalonians, the Johannine letters and a couple of OT bonus passages. In my current setup that’s 54 distinct prophecies.
  2. Loads all the subjects
    YAML files in data/subjects/ describe each candidate:
    • Basic bio (name, era, role)
    • A 0.0-1.0 rating for how well they match each prophecy
    • Short evidence notes explaining the rating
  3. Assigns a very skeptical prior
    By default, the prior is one in a billion that any given person is the Antichrist.
  4. Apply Bayes' theorem
    For each prophecy, we define:
    • How likely it would be if someone was the Antichrist
    • How likely it would be if they weren’t

Then we scale that by the subject’s rating for that prophecy and get a likelihood ratio:

LR = P(evidence | Antichrist) / P(evidence | Not Antichrist)

Multiply all those LRs together (in log space), apply Bayes’ theorem, and you get a posterior probability and a log likelihood ratio for each subject.

  1. Generates tables, summaries, and plots
    The script prints a ranking table and, if you pass --plot, generates bar charts and a prophecy heatmap.

Nero

The single strongest prophecy match in the entire dataset is this one:

Revelation 13:18 – The Antichrist’s number will be 666, which requires wisdom to interpret.

In the model, that prophecy has a log10 likelihood ratio of 156.88 for Nero, because:

  • Hebrew gematria for “Nero Caesar” famously adds up to 666.
  • This is not a conspiracy theory; it’s been the default reading for a long time by Biblical scholars.

On top of that, Nero:

  • Ruled Rome, the city on seven hills, right in the period Revelation is usually dated.
  • Demanded worship, built colossal statues, and loved blasphemous titles.
  • Killed Christians in extremely creative and horrifying ways (human torches, arena games).
  • Oversaw the Jewish War that ended with the destruction of the Temple.

So when you feed all the prophecies through Bayes, Nero comes out with:

  • Prophecy coverage: 100%
  • Log10 likelihood ratio: 12.4
  • Posterior probability: effectively 100%

Donald Trump

The biggest LR boost for Trump was Revelation 13:3-5 (LR: 94.29)

3 And I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast.
4 And they worshipped the dragon which gave power unto the beast: and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? who is able to make war with him?
5 And there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and power was given unto him to continue forty and two months.
  • "And I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death"
    • A bullet literally tore through Trump’s ear, but the intended wound was fatal in the ordinary meaning. It simply missed by centimeters.
  • “The whole world was amazed”
    • The clip went instantly global and dominated social media worldwide.
  • “And they followed the Beast”
    • His approval shot up afterward and fundraising spiked.
  • "And power was given unto him to continue forty and two months"
    • 42 months is 3.5 years. Eerily close to a 4 year presidential term.
    • Trump was sworn in again on January 20, 2025.
    • 42 months after that is Thursday, July 20, 2028.
    • If something happens to Trump around July 2028 then I might have to start going to church again.

Here are the full results of the analysis for Donald Trump:

  • Prior Probability: 0.0000001% (1 in a billion)
  • Log10 Likelihood Ratio: 12.5 (0.1 higher than Nero)
    • An LR of 12.5 means the evidence is 3 trillion times more likely if Donald Trump matches the prophetic-hypothesis profile than if it does not
  • Posterior Probability: effectively 100%
